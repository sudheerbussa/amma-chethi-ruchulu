# Campaign A — Customers (orders)

**Objective:** Drive WhatsApp food orders for lunch/dinner slots.  
**Audience:** Households, WFH, office people, NRIs ordering for parents in-area (secondary).  
**Primary CTA:** Order on WhatsApp → `{{WA_LINK}}`  
**Secondary CTA:** Save for today’s cutoff · Share with family group.

**Tone:** Warm, proud, local, clean — never desperate discounts in every post.

## Channel packing (same creative DNA)

| Platform | Poster | Video |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Instagram | 1:1 or 4:5 feed | 9:16 Reels 15–30s |
| Facebook | 1:1 feed | 9:16 or 1:1 Reels/FB video |
| YouTube | Community image optional | Shorts 9:16 (same reel); optional 16:9 Long if you stitch story later |

**Caption length:** IG shorter; FB slightly longer; YT Shorts text on-screen + short description with link.
