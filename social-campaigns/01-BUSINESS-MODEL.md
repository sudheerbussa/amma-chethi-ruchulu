# Amma Chethi Ruchulu — Business Model (for ads & Cursor)

Use this as the **source of truth** for campaign claims, captions, and prompts. If a creative contradicts this file, fix the creative.

---

## 1. One-line pitch

**Amma Chethi Ruchulu** brings **home-cooked, signature dishes from local homemakers (“Ammas”)** to customers via **scheduled WhatsApp orders**, with **collection, hub packing, and delivery** handled by the platform — not restaurant clouds or dark kitchens posing as home food.

---

## 2. Problem → solution

### Customer side


| Pain                                        | Our answer                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outside food feels oily / generic           | Named Amma’s specialty dishes; home-style taste                              |
| Don’t know who cooked it                    | Menu shows **dish + Amma name**                                              |
| Instant-delivery apps push speed over trust | **Pre-order / cut-off** model so cooks plan, food isn’t rushed fake “30 min” |
| Want local, not chain                       | Hyperlocal cooks + fixed lunch/dinner windows                                |


### Cook (Amma) side


| Pain                                | Our answer                                                                |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cook well but no customer funnel    | We bring orders; she cooks sold capacity only                             |
| Hate delivery / packaging logistics | We **collect** from home → **hub pack** → **riders** deliver              |
| Unclear money                       | **~70% of food gross** on her lines (configurable); weekly payouts design |
| Fear wasting food                   | **Portion caps** per meal; sold-out when full                             |


---

## 3. Value chain (what we actually do)

```text
Customer orders on WhatsApp (ahead of cutoff)
        ↓
Amma cooks at home (sold portion count)
        ↓
Platform COLLECTS from Amma homes
        ↓
HUB: QC, pack, label, batch routes
        ↓
RIDERS deliver from hub (not multi-stop cook pickup for riders)
```

**Important for ads:** We are **not** “Swiggy but cheaper only.” We are **home specialists + ops layer** (quality, pack, last mile). That trust story is the brand.

## 4. Product: meals & timing


| Meal        | Customer order discipline                                                                       | Delivery idea (campaign language) |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Lunch**   | Order by morning cutoff (ops: ~10:00 IST bot / plan may differ — **publish your live cutoffs**) | Midday / lunch window             |
| **Dinner**  | Order by afternoon cutoff (ops: ~16:00 IST bot)                                                 | Evening window                    |
| Late orders | **Next-day / next-slot pre-order** — never fake live “now”                                      | “Plan tomorrow’s lunch with Amma” |


**Ads must say:** scheduled / pre-order / order-by-time — **not** “delivered in 20 minutes.”

---

## 5. Money (simple, campaign-safe)


| Who               | What they get                                                           |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customer pays** | Dish price + delivery (+ packaging if any)                              |
| **Amma**          | Majority of **food** portion (~70% default of food gross on her dishes) |
| **Platform**      | Rest of food margin + delivery contribution − ops                       |


**Do say (chef ads):** “Earn from the kitchen you already run.” “We handle orders, packing logistics, delivery.” “You cook; we bring the customers.”

**Don’t say unless legally true in writing:** salary, government scheme, “guaranteed ₹X/month,” FSSAI already done for every kitchen (recruiters should be honest about onboarding steps).

---

## 6. USP elements (repeat in creatives)

1. **Named cooks** — “Tomato Pappu · Lakshmi Amma,” not anonymous cloud kitchen.
2. **Home kitchen authenticity** — who cooked it, specialty taste, steamer / home stove craft (visuals may use banana leaf plating for *appetite*, not as the delivery vessel).
3. **WhatsApp-native** — no “download our app first” for MVP; order in chat.
4. **Capacity honesty** — limited portions; when full, full.
5. **Hub care** — food is checked and sealed in **good-quality disposable, food-grade boxes** before it reaches the door.

### Packaging decision (ops truth — do not romanticise steel dabbas for delivery)


| Layer                | What we use                                                                                           | Why                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Amma kitchen**     | Normal cookware (steel kadhai, pressure cooker, etc.)                                                 | Real home cooking                                                       |
| **Customer handoff** | **New disposable food-grade boxes** (leak-resistant, decent lid seal; upgrade grade as volume allows) | Hygiene, no shared tiffin returns, safe for hub + rider, predictable QC |
| **Hub**              | Steel **tables** / clean surfaces for packing — not “returnable dabba fleet”                          | Ops hygiene, not product packaging                                      |


**Do not change the business model for this.** The model is: named home cooks + collect + hub pack + deliver + WhatsApp pre-order. Packaging is *how* you deliver safely, not *what* you sell.

**Ads should show:** sealed clean packs with labels (trust/hygiene hero posts) **and** food plated for beauty (desire posts). Avoid implying the meal arrives in lifelong steel dabbas unless you actually run a return-and-wash system (you are not).

## 8. Two campaigns, two promises

### Campaign A — Customers

- Promise: **Real home food on your schedule, from Ammas nearby.**
- CTA: **Order on WhatsApp** · see today’s / tomorrow’s lunch & dinner.
- Emotion: nostalgia, trust, “Amma’s hand,” clean plates, family meal.

### Campaign B — Home chefs

- Promise: **Turn your signature dishes into weekly earnings — without becoming a restaurant.**
- CTA: **Apply / WhatsApp us to onboard** (slot + areas + dishes).
- Emotion: respect for skill, dignity of home work, independence, flexible batch cooking.

**Never mix CTAs in one ad** (e.g. don’t recruit chefs on a pure customer food porn post). Different content series, different UTM/caption tags if boosting.

## 9. Claims hygiene (list for reviewers)

Safe:

- Home-style / made by local homemakers
- Order via WhatsApp; lunch & dinner slots
- Limited portions per Amma
- Platform collects, packs, delivers
- Cooks share majority of food revenue (say “majority” or “about 70%” if you commit)

Risky / avoid unless verified:

- “Organic / farm-to-table always”
- Medical claims (“low oil cures BP”)
- Competitor baseless attacks
- Exact earnings for every cook
- “Delivery in X minutes” as a hard guarantee
- Using real Amma faces without release / consent

## 10. Localisation placeholders (what each number means)

You run **two separate WhatsApp numbers**. Never put both CTAs on one ad.

| Role | Who contacts it | Used on |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| **Customer order line** | People who want food (menu, order, support for orders) | Campaign A posters, reels, bios “Order” |
| **Recruitment / chef line** | Homemakers applying to cook as Ammas | Campaign B posters, reels, bios “Cook with us” — ops owner (friend) |

Do **not** send chef applicants to the food-order number (or the reverse).

### Token glossary

| Token | What it is | When it appears on creative |
|-------|------------|-------------------------------|
| `{{WA_LINK}}` | **Clickable order link** → food WhatsApp (prefilled “today’s menu”) | **Default for customers:** captions, bio, link-in-bio, QR codes, end cards (“Order on WhatsApp”) |
| `{{PHONE}}` | Digits for that **same** customer line | **Only if** you choose to print/show the number on that creative. Otherwise **omit** — no need to expose the number. |
| `{{CHEF_WA}}` | Clickable **recruitment** link → ops WhatsApp for Amma applications | All Campaign B creatives (link, QR, captions) |
| `{{CHEF_PHONE}}` | Digits for that **same** recruitment line | Only if a chef creative needs visible digits; otherwise link/QR is enough |
| Keyword **AMMA** | Prefill on `{{CHEF_WA}}` so chats open tagged for recruit | Chef ads only |

**Customer policy (locked):**  
Always CTA = **WhatsApp** via link or QR (`{{WA_LINK}}`).  
Show `{{PHONE}}` **only** when that specific poster/video/flyer is designed to display the number. Most digital posts = link/QR only, digits hidden.

**Mental model**

```text
{{WA_LINK}}     = tap/QR  →  CUSTOMER WhatsApp   (use on almost every food ad)
{{PHONE}}       = typed digits of customer line  (use only when you display the number)
{{CHEF_WA}}     = tap/QR  →  RECRUITMENT WhatsApp (ops: 8886128995)
{{CHEF_PHONE}}  = typed digits of recruitment    (only when you display that number)
```
