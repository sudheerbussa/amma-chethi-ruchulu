# Campaign B — Home chef / Amma recruiting

**Objective:** Onboard homemakers who cook known signature dishes.  
**Audience:** 30–60 age women (primary), home cooks of any gender who run strong kitchens; Telugu / local-language heavy OK.  
**Primary CTA:** WhatsApp keyword **AMMA** or `{{CHEF_WA}}` · “We cook · you collect · we pay share of food.”  
**Tone:** Respect, partnership, clarity — never “side hustle bro” or “get rich.”

---

## Content pillars (10 posts)

| Pillar | Posts | Job |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Dignity / invitation | H01, H07 | “Your kitchen already has value” |

| Money story | H03, H08 | Fair share ~70% food; capacity-based |

## Channel packing

Same as customers: **poster + 9:16 video** first; tailor captions for respect + bilingual if needed.

**Comment pinning:** Pin “WhatsApp AMMA + area + 1 specialty dish” instruction.

---

## Objections to address in comments (not every ad)

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| Do I need a restaurant? | No — home kitchen partnership model |
| Who pays riders? | Platform ops |
| What if food leftover? | Cook to sold orders + caps |
| Food licence / FSSAI | Be honest about what you require; don’t overclaim in ads |
