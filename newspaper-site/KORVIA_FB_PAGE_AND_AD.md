## 4. Ads: Business Manager vs Boost

Do **not** use the blue **Boost post** button. Use **Ads Manager**.

- If you already have a **Meta Business** (Amma Chethi Ruchulu): **Business settings → Accounts → Pages → Add** the Korvia Page. Use the **same ad account**, new campaign.  
- If not: open [adsmanager.facebook.com](https://adsmanager.facebook.com) → it will create an ad account on your Facebook login. Pay with **your** UPI/card (not ACR’s food entity if you want clean books).

Turn on **WhatsApp** in the ad: Page linked + WhatsApp number verified on that phone.
