## Compliance / creative tips for Meta

- Prefer **Click to WhatsApp** campaign objective.  
- Don’t claim “guaranteed sales” or fake testimonials.  
- Avoid using official WhatsApp logo artwork (Meta often flags it); show generic chat UI or phone-in-hand.  
- First comment pin: “WhatsApp ‘DEMO’ to +91 80552 92935”  
- Start with **Card 1 + Card 5** as a 2-card test if budget is small, then full 5-card carousel.
